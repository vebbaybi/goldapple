# Legal Review Register

This is an engineering issue register, not legal advice. Qualified counsel must review: Apache-2.0 notices and contribution terms; Golden Apple names, marks, logo and generated artwork; privacy notices and cross-border AI processing; source-code confidentiality; provider terms; security research policy; third-party agent and marketplace licensing; publisher payouts; consumer protection; tax; sanctions; KYC/AML; custody; money transmission; token classification and securities/commodities implications; treasury and governance; and jurisdiction-specific employment/monitoring risks.

No token, wallet, marketplace, payout, custody, or public network should be implemented or marketed before the applicable entries have named owners, jurisdictions, decisions, and approval evidence.
