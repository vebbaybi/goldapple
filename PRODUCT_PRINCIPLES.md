# Product Principles

These principles are normative and ordered. Safety and user control override convenience.

1. **Local first.** Process and persist locally whenever practical.
2. **Evidence before assertion.** Label observations, inferences, hypotheses, and recommendations.
3. **Read before write.** Observation is the first operating mode.
4. **Least privilege.** Grant the smallest capability for the shortest useful duration.
5. **Explicit authorization.** Permission is scoped by workspace, source, action, destination, and time.
6. **Reversible operations.** Prefer preview, simulation, backup, and rollback.
7. **Verification before success claims.** Exit status alone is insufficient when effects can be checked.
8. **Security and privacy by design.** Threat modeling and minimization precede implementation.
9. **User sovereignty.** Users own their data, history, provider choice, and deletion controls.
10. **No silent cloud upload.** External transmission requires an intelligible boundary and consent.
11. **Provider independence.** OpenAI-first does not mean OpenAI-coupled domain logic.
12. **No unverifiable diagnosis.** Unsupported conclusions are explicitly uncertain.
13. **Graceful degradation.** Missing providers or evidence reduce capability, not integrity.
14. **Observable and reproducible operations.** Material work produces structured audit events.
15. **Secure defaults and explicit trust boundaries.** Workspace data is adversarial until proven otherwise.
16. **Blockchain only where useful.** Public verification or settlement must justify its cost and exposure.
17. **No token-driven decisions.** No token is required for product utility or MVP adoption.
18. **Architecture before autonomy.** Permissions, simulation, and verification precede write access.
