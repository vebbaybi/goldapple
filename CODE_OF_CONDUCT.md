# Code of Conduct

We commit to a respectful, harassment-free community across identity, experience, and background. Be specific, assume good faith while requiring evidence, protect private information, and critique work rather than people. Harassment, threats, doxxing, discriminatory conduct, and deliberate disruption are unacceptable.

Enforcement contact and escalation ownership must be established before public community launch. Until then, do not solicit sensitive private reports through an undocumented channel. Maintainers will document proportionate corrective actions and avoid retaliation.
