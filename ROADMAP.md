# Roadmap

No completion dates are promised. Advancement requires the exit evidence defined for each phase.

| Phase | Scope | Exit evidence | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Foundation, architecture, requirements, threat model, public-site foundation | Reviewed documents, traceability, validation | In progress |
| 1 | VSIX shell and authenticated local-runtime handshake | Signed dev build, trust-gated IPC tests | Planned |
| 2 | Read-only workspace discovery and evidence store | Fixture-based discovery and privacy tests | Planned |
| 3 | Git, dependency, diagnostic, build/test evidence adapters | Adapter contract and integration tests | Planned |
| 4 | Investigation and cited finding pipeline | Golden scenario produces reproducible findings | Planned MVP |
| 5 | VSIX V1 hardening and release engineering | Security review, release gates, support policy | Planned |
| 6 | Controlled, previewable actions | Permission, simulation, rollback, verification evidence | Research |
| 7 | Native runtime and broader system evidence | Platform threat model and portable adapters | Research |
| 8 | Golden Agent Protocol and SDK | Interoperability and provenance specification | Research |
| 9 | Optional network and marketplace | Legal, security, economic, and abuse reviews | Research |
| 10 | Proof of Useful Work experiments | Sybil-resistant measured prototypes | Research |
| 11 | Progressive decentralization | Governance readiness evidence | Research |
